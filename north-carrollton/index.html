Held as two awards

The $553,730 Small Business Development Center award is to North Central Planning and Development District. The $162,378 DRA street award is to the Town of North Carrollton. Different recipient, different program, different day. They are not the same money.

Packager vs recipient

NCPDD is the Local Development District. Most community awards in this file are written to the town or county. Bank of Winona CDFI awards are institutional receipts at 312 N Applegate, Winona, not town-hall awards.
